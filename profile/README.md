# agenthouse

### Making AI work. Across the enterprise.

**Open-source technology. AI enablement. Enterprise solutions.**

AI creates opportunities across every level of an organization. Turning those opportunities into measurable value requires more than technology.

At **agenthouse**, we build open-source tools, develop agentic applications, and help organizations integrate AI into everyday operations.

Our mission is to make AI practical, accessible, and sustainable — from individual productivity to enterprise-wide transformation.

## Enterprise services

We complement our open-source ecosystem with professional services and managed AI solutions.

- **AI Strategy & Enablement** — Align leadership, build AI capabilities, and accelerate adoption throughout the organization.
- **Agentic Applications** — Design and integrate intelligent agents into real business processes.
- **AI Engineering** — Implement secure, maintainable, and interoperable AI solutions.
- **Governance & Operations** — Establish oversight, reliability, and continuous improvement for AI-powered workflows.

**Our approach:** Start with business value. Build on existing systems. Keep people accountable. Avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in.
